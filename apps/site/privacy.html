Privacy

GrammarMe checks your writing inside your browser. We do not run accounts, analytics or ads, and we never see what you write.

Grammar and spelling checks

The checker runs on your computer. Your text is not uploaded, logged or stored anywhere except your own browser.

What is kept in your browser

  • Your settings, personal dictionary and turned-off rules.
  • Your current draft, if “Keep my draft in this browser” is on. You can delete it in Settings at any time.

The optional AI check

It is off until you turn it on and add your own server in Settings. Text is sent only when you press AI check, up to 4,000 characters at a time, to that server and then to the AI provider it is set up with. Each provider has its own terms; the project notes compare them.

The Chrome extension

The extension checks text boxes on the pages you visit, on your computer. It skips password, one-time-code and payment fields and sends nothing anywhere.

Fonts

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. The editor itself loads no third-party fonts.
